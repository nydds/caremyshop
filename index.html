Questions, answered plainly

        
Frequently asked questions

      

      

        

          Do you need my Google password?
          
Never. You invite CareMyShop as a Manager on your Google Business Profile from your own account, and you can remove that access in one click at any time. We will never ask for your password — not by email, not by phone, not in person.

        

        

          What happens with a bad review — do you just post whatever?
          
No. Every 1–3★ review is drafted and sent to you for approval before anything is posted. We never argue with a customer online and never admit legal fault. You decide the tone; we make sure it sounds like you on your best day.

        

        

          Will the replies sound like a robot wrote them?
          
No. Every reply quotes a specific detail from the customer’s review — the dish they ordered, the person who helped them, the wait they endured — and matches your shop’s voice: calm and trustworthy for a clinic, warm and hospitable for a restaurant, straightforward and reliable for an auto shop. One-line templates are banned here.

        

        

          What happens after the 30-day free pilot?
          
Nothing happens unless you say so. On day 30 we ask; if you stay, the pilot converts to $39/mo, locked for life. If you cancel, care stops and nothing is charged. There is no card charged during the pilot itself.

        

        

          Can I cancel anytime? Are there hidden fees?
          
Yes to canceling anytime, no to hidden fees. All plans are month to month (except the optional 3-month Plus commitment, which waives the $149 setup). The price on this page is the price you pay — no ad spend, no per-reply charges, no setup surprises.

        

        

          What’s in the monthly competitor snapshot?
          
A branded one-page scorecard: your review count and rating change for the month, your reply rate vs. three nearby shops, and one concrete action tip for next month — for example, which question to answer on your profile, or which photo to refresh.
